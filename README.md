# sproos! Rain & Rinse PDP mockups

Round 1 creative prototypes for the Rain & Rinse product page. Three directions, one review shell.

- `index.html` – review shell: switch directions, toggle phone view, read review notes
- `direction-1.html` – Two sides of your shower (recommended main PDP)
- `direction-2.html` – The spa upgrade for your actual bathroom
- `direction-3.html` – See what a second head adds (1 vs 2 ad traffic)
- `assets/config.js` – prices, filter cost, colors and trust terms. Edit here, all pages update.
- `assets/pdp.js` – shared header, purchase module, FAQs, sticky bar
- `assets/pdp.css` – shared styles

Link to a direction directly with `index.html#d2`.

Prototype only. No checkout or external services. Striped boxes are photo placeholders labeled with the intended shot.
